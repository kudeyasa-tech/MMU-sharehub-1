Before you upload

A few small checks save everyone time.

  • Keep the file under 20 MB.
  • Use a format the library accepts. Executables are blocked.
  • Remove your student ID and anything private.
  • Only upload work you made or can share.

A better title goes a long way

Put the unit, the exam and the useful bit in the title.

good: Database Systems — Normalization + SQL, Final 2025

Less helpful: “DB notes final final v2”. I have seen about six of those.
