Before you upload

Small checks now save time later.

  • Keep the file under 20 MB.
  • Use one of the supported document or image formats.
  • Remove personal information you do not want to share.
  • Only upload work you made or have permission to share.

A better title goes a long way

Include the unit, the exam period and what makes the note useful.

Good: “Database Systems — Normalization + SQL Final Revision (Trimester 2)”

Less helpful: “DB notes final final v2”.
