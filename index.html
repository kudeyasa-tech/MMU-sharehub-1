How it works

Three steps. Less panic.

The library is deliberately boring to use. Find a note, look inside, and leave one of your own when you are done.

01

Find your unit

Search by subject, faculty or year. You can also type the name of the student who shared it. No account needed for any of this.

02

Open the file first

Preview opens in a new tab. Check that the note covers your syllabus before you pull it onto your laptop.

03

Leave yours behind

Log in, upload the tidy version of your notes, and add a title a stranger can understand. That is the whole trade.

Pay it forward

Your notes could save someone a week of panic.

You already did the hard work. Upload the clean version, add a short description, and let the next batch find it.
