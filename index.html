How it works

Three steps. Less panic.

ShareHub is intentionally simple: find what you need, check it first, then pass yours on.

Find your unit

Search by subject or narrow the library down by faculty and year. No account needed.

Preview the notes

Open the file in a new tab first, so you know it covers the right topics before downloading.

Pass it forward

Log in, upload your notes and help someone else walk into the exam a little calmer.

Pay it forward

Your notes could save someone a whole semester.

You already did the hard work. Upload the clean version, add a helpful description, and let the next batch benefit.
